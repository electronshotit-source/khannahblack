The Studio

Photography, genre fluid — a story told frame by frame.

khannahblack is the studio of Kester Kanayo Onyemaechi, known to his clients simply as Kanayo. His work moves fluidly between weddings, portraiture, events, and lifestyle — always in search of the quiet, luxurious moment hiding inside the ordinary one.

Every frame is composed with intention: rich tones, natural light, and an eye trained to find elegance in real emotion. From intimate portrait sessions to full wedding coverage, khannahblack delivers imagery that feels timeless.
